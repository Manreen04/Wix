🌟 Website Overview
The Kenny Hills Bakers website is designed to be clean, friendly, and easy to navigate. Here's what you can find:

Home Section:
A warm welcome message explaining the purpose of the bakery—delicious pastries and top-tier service.

Menu / Products Section:
A wide range of items neatly listed by category:
Cakes (e.g., Red Velvet, Tiramisu – RM91)
Breads (Baguette, Brioche – RM7 for 2)
Puddings, Cookies, Pastries, Cupcakes
Baking Supplies & Utensils

Best Sellers Highlighted:
Quick view of popular items like Red Velvet Cake and Java Chip Cookies.

Contact Page:
Address, Phone number, Email, Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok), Map for location, Contact form for customer messages

Customer Feedback Section:
A form link where users can leave reviews and read feedback.

Promotions & Club Membership:
Christmas Sale (up to 50% off with terms)
Free gifts and offers for cookies & baking supplies
Join the Kenny Hills Club for discounts

Quick Links for Navigation: 
Menu, Order Online, Delivery, About, Contact, Book a Table, and more.
